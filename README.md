<h2>About me</h2>
<p>I am a PhD researcher at <strong>Warwick Medical School, University of Warwick</strong>, with a background in public health. My doctoral research examines type 2 diabetes in resource-limited settings using large-scale population data, interpretable machine learning, multilevel epidemiological methods, and health economic modelling.</p>
<p>I am particularly interested in how transparent models perform against simpler decision rules, how findings generalise across populations, and how quantitative research can support healthcare decisions under resource constraints.</p>

      <h3>01 · Diabetes risk stratification</h3>
      <p><strong>South Africa | Fast-and-frugal decision trees</strong></p>
      <p>Evaluated non-invasive screening rules using nationally representative survey data. In the hold-out sample, the balanced-screening tree achieved 92% sensitivity; its balanced accuracy was similar to an age-only rule.</p>
